# Phase 05 – Planet Feed and Moderation

## Goal

Cho phép chọn nội dung để chia sẻ ra cộng đồng.

## Prompt

1. Post, comment, reply, reaction, save.
2. Publish from journal bằng hành động rõ.
3. Planet member policy.
4. Cursor pagination.
5. Rule-based feed score baseline.
6. Diversity rule.
7. Report flow.
8. Moderator đúng planet.
9. Block/mute minimal nếu trong P0.
10. Abuse tests.
11. UI loading/empty/error.
12. Audit moderation.

## Stop condition

Không tự động làm journal public khi tạo post.
